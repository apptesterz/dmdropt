## 2. Definitions

- **Software** — the dmdrop application, in source and built form, including documentation, configuration, setup tooling, and any Updates provided.
- **Licence Key** — the signed key issued to Licensee identifying their Edition, Account Limit, and Update Period.
- **Edition** — the tier purchased (Personal, Pro, or Agency).
- **Account Limit** — the maximum number of Instagram accounts that may be connected, determined by Edition.
- **Update Period** — twelve (12) months from the date of purchase, unless extended by a renewal purchase.
- **Instance** — one deployment of the Software operated by Licensee.

## 3. Grant of licence

Subject to this Agreement and payment in full, Licensor grants Licensee a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, worldwide, perpetual** licence to:

a. install and operate the Software on infrastructure Licensee controls;
b. connect up to the Account Limit of Instagram accounts **owned or lawfully administered by Licensee**;
c. modify the Software for Licensee's own internal use.

The licence is perpetual: the version of the Software received continues to work after the Update Period ends. Nothing is disabled and no functionality is removed when the Update Period expires.

**Editions:**

| Edition | Account Limit |
|---|---|
| Personal | 1 |
| Pro | 3 |
| Agency | Unlimited |

One licence covers one Licensee. A licence is not shared between separate businesses.

Licensee must not, and must not permit any third party to:

a. distribute, publish, resell, rent, lease, lend, or sublicense the Software, in source or built form, whether modified or not, and whether or not for payment;

d. share, resell, publish, or transfer a Licence Key;

## 6. Licence key, build identifiers, and activation

**Licensee is expressly informed and agrees that:**

a. Each copy of the Software is **individually identifiable**. Builds contain identifiers derived from Licensee's purchase, enabling Licensor to determine the origin of any copy found distributed in breach of clause 5. These identifiers do not affect functionality and must not be removed.

b. The Software may contact a Licensor endpoint on installation and when checking for Updates, transmitting only the Licence Key and a non-personal instance identifier. This is used to detect licence misuse.

c. **Verification never blocks operation.** If the Licensor endpoint is unreachable, or Licensee's Instance is offline, the Software continues to function normally. Licence verification is performed offline against a signature and requires no network access.

d. No campaign content, message content, Instagram data, follower data, or access token is ever transmitted to Licensor. See clause 9.
